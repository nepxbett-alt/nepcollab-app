# NepCollab — Launch marketing kit

**Live:** https://nepcollab.vercel.app  
**One-liner:** Nepal’s marketplace for creators and brands to find each other and collaborate—without middlemen holding your money.

## Positioning
- **Not** an agency. **Not** a payment app.
- Structured campaigns → applications → collaboration → delivery.
- Payments stay between brand and creator (bank, eSewa, Khalti, product).

## Audience
1. Creators in Kathmandu, Pokhara, and major cities (fashion, food, travel, tech, fitness).
2. Brand marketers at D2C, retail, fintech, telco, hospitality.

## Sample story (live on platform)
**Daraz Finds — My Week in Five Products** × **Ananya Karki**  
- Application accepted  
- Collaboration **active**  
- Thread with real back-and-forth on brief and shoot plan  

Use this as a demo path when showing investors or brands.
